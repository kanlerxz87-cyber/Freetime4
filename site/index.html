When is the San Francisco radio actually quiet?

A minute is free when none of these are on air on receivable Bay Area AM/FM radio: MLB (all 30 clubs; Giants & A's flagged high priority), NFL San Francisco 49ers (preseason/regular/postseason), Stanford & Cal football, and Westwood One national NFL broadcasts (TNF/SNF/MNF/playoffs/Super Bowl on KNBR 680/104.5).

Executive summary — the clear answer. Each year has exactly one long quiet stretch and a set of smaller ones:
  • The big window: mid-January/February to late March. With MLB spring training counted (it is MLB preseason), the fully-free stretch is Jan 31 – Feb 13, 2027 (14 straight days), Jan 30 – Feb 12, 2028 and Jan 28 – Feb 10, 2029 (estimates) — between the NFL playoffs/Super Bowl and the first spring games. If you ignore spring-training games (rarely carried on Bay Area AM/FM), the window grows to Feb 15 – Mar 23, 2027 (37 days), Feb 14 – Mar 21, 2028 and Feb 12 – Mar 20, 2029 (estimates) — enough for 1, 2 or 3 weeks off.
  • The All-Star break (mid-July): 3–4 days with zero MLB, e.g. around July 13, 2027 (official All-Star date, Wrigley Field).
  • After the World Series ends (late Oct/early Nov): the first weeks of November are NFL-byes-free only some days — see the vacation-windows page; November 2026 opens with a 7-day fully-free week (Nov 1–7).
  • Never free: April–September weekdays have daily MLB somewhere (all 30 clubs count), and September–January layers NFL/NCAAF on top.
