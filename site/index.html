FREE TIME VACATION

When can you take a trip and not miss a single game you actually care about? A minute counts as busy when a live broadcast you can hear on an AM/FM radio in Outer Sunset, San Francisco (94122) is on air. Every other minute is free. Times are Pacific.

Two answers, both shown. Strict = no MLB game anywhere (all 30 clubs), no NFL game, no Stanford/Cal game and no Westwood One national broadcast. Audible = only what your radio can actually pick up in 94122 (Giants on KNBR, Athletics on KNEW 960, 49ers on KSAN/KNBR, Stanford on KNBR, Cal on KSFO 810). The audible answer is the one to plan around; the strict answer is the worst case.

What counts as “busy”

High priority — never miss

  • MLB — San Francisco Giants (flagship KNBR 680)
  • MLB — Athletics (Bay Area affiliate KNEW 960)
  • NFL — San Francisco 49ers, preseason + regular season + postseason (official radio: KSAN 107.7 FM / KNBR 104.5 FM / 680 AM)
  • NCAAF — Stanford (KNBR)
  • NCAAF — California Golden Bears (official card: KSFO 810 AM, Big Game on KNBR)
  • Every Westwood One national NFL broadcast (TNF, MNF, SNF, Thanksgiving, Christmas, Saturday doubleheaders, playoffs)

Shown but normal priority

All 30 MLB clubs, every NFL game, and every other sport on the Westwood One grid (NCAAF, USMNT/USWNT, NCAA baseball, lacrosse) are listed and can be counted in the strict answer, because a national broadcast can still be on your dial.

Games whose start time isn't published are held at the most common slot and marked Estimated. Nothing is guessed silently.
