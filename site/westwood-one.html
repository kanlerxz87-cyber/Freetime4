Westwood One Sports — national NFL radio (Bay Area: KNBR 680/104.5)

Westwood One is the national radio home of the NFL: Thursday, Sunday and Monday night football, holiday tripleheaders, Saturday special doubleheaders, playoffs and the Super Bowl. In San Francisco these broadcasts air on KNBR 680 AM / 104.5 FM (1050 KTCT carries overflow).

Resolved discrepancy (previously flagged by two sessions): Westwood One's listed times are the radio broadcast window start (pregame), not kickoff. Verified against the official schedule feed: Dec 17, 2026 — WW1 on air 4:30 PM PT, kickoff 5:15 PM PT (8:15 PM ET); Jan 3, 2027 — WW1 on air 4:30 PM PT, kickoff 5:20 PM PT (8:20 PM ET). Both "conflicts" were pregame starts, not contradictions.
