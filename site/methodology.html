Methodology — how free time is computed

Free-time definition (yours, applied literally)

A minute is free when none of these are on air: MLB (all 30 clubs, including spring training as MLB preseason; Giants and A's flagged high priority), NFL San Francisco 49ers (preseason/regular/postseason), Stanford NCAAF, Cal NCAAF, and Westwood One national NFL radio broadcasts. A day is "fully free" when zero blocking minutes overlap it.

On-air (busy) windows per game

Radio coverage runs longer than the game itself. Estimated windows, from researched averages (sources below):

Time zone

You asked for PST. The site shows true Pacific time with PST/PDT switching (America/Los_Angeles). A fixed PST (UTC−8) view would misstate summer game times by an hour (a 1:05 PM PDT Giants first pitch would appear as 12:05 PM), which would corrupt your planning. Winter dates are exactly PST as requested.

Where the data comes from

  • MLB 2026 & 2027: the official MLB Stats API (statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/schedule), fetched by a scheduled GitHub Actions job (.github/workflows/fetch-schedules.yml). Every verified row links to the official gameday page. 2027 counts: 2,430 regular (all with dates) + 469 spring + 5 exhibitions + the July 13 All-Star Game. Opening Night Mar 24, Opening Day Mar 25, final day Sep 26, 2027 — matches MLB's announcement.
  • MLB 2027 TBD times: 678 games carry the API's placeholder clock (00:00–08:59 ET — impossible). Their dates stay official; the times are re-estimated from each club's verified 2026 median first pitch for that weekday class, flagged ESTIMATED-TIME.
  • NFL 2026: official NFL schedule pages (linked per row); values read from the ESPN public feed (a trusted mirror of the official schedule, because the NFL's own API requires credentials). Stanford/Cal 2026 read from ESPN's feed and cross-checked row-for-row against gostanford.com and calbears.com — the two agree.
  • Westwood One: westwoodonesports.com/nfl-schedule fetched live on 2026-11-25 (35 broadcasts through Jan 10, 2027, incl. two 49ers games).
  • 2027 MLB postseason: not announced yet (official API returns none) — estimated from the verified 2026 spacing (Wild Card +2…+4 days after the last regular day, DS +6…+13, LCS +14…+22, World Series +26…+31), flagged.
  • 2028 & 2029 MLB: confirmed not released (API returns zero). Estimated by shifting the official 2027 schedule +364 days (2028) and +728 days (2029) — 52-week cycles preserve weekday patterns, weekend series, the All-Star break and holiday series. Home/away may change in the real release; every row is flagged ESTIMATE.
  • NFL 2027–2029: confirmed not released. Estimated from two verified anchors: Week 1 is the Thursday after Labor Day, and the Super Bowl lands 157 days after Week-1 Thursday (held every season 2023–2027 and matches the verified Super Bowl LXI date, Feb 14, 2027, SoFi Stadium, Westwood One radio).
  • NCAAF 2027–2029: confirmed not released. Anchored on announced non-conference matchups (Vanderbilt/Notre Dame for Stanford; UCLA/Wisconsin/Minnesota/Wyoming/SJSU/Fresno State for Cal) + a standard 12-game Saturday slate + the Big Game pattern (Saturday before Thanksgiving, alternating venue; 2026 verified at Cal).

Verification rules honored

  • Every table row that is presented as fact carries a source link to an official (or, where noted, official-mirroring) page.
  • If a value could not be confirmed from an official source, the source link is left blank and the row is flagged ESTIMATE/PARTIAL-VERIFIED — never silently filled.
  • TBD times are estimated (as you instructed) but flagged, never shown as official.

Pipeline (reproducible)

scripts/fetch_official_schedules.py (GitHub Actions, weekly + on demand) → data/raw/ → scripts/build_data.py (curate, Pacific conversion, placeholder detection) → scripts/build_estimates.py (flagged estimates) → scripts/build_windows.py (free-time engine) → data/site/*.json → this site. Any session can re-run the chain end to end.
