NFL — 49ers (high priority) & national windows

Every San Francisco 49ers game (preseason, regular season, postseason) is high priority and airs on Bay Area radio (KNBR 680/104.5, 107.7 The Bone, 810 KSFO). Other clubs' games are shown but are display-only — they are not receivable on Bay Area AM/FM unless carried by Westwood One (see Westwood One page, which carries the radio-blocking windows).

Why the display-only rows matter: your definition blocks only 49ers games + Westwood One national broadcasts + MLB + Stanford/Cal. Non-49ers NFL games not on Westwood One are shown so you can see the full league schedule, but they do not affect your free time.

2027–2029 seasons are not released (confirmed empty via official feeds on 2026-11-25) — estimated rows are pattern-based (Week 1 = Thursday after Labor Day; Super Bowl = Week 1 + 157 days, a rule that held for the 2023–2027 seasons and matches the verified SB LXI date of Feb 14, 2027).

Verified rows: NFL.com schedule pages (official), values cross-checked against ESPN public schedule feed. Playoff slots show verified windows with teams TBD.
