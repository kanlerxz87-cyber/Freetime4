Executive summary

The short version: take the trip in the January–mid-February dead zone. It is the only stretch of the year where your radio is quiet for weeks, and it happens in all three years you asked about. Everything else in the sports year is a 4–6 day window at best.

2. Why January–February is the answer

The calendar leaves a hole every year

  • MLB spring training does not start until the third week of February.
  • The NFL regular season ends the first week of January; the Super Bowl is the only game left, and it is on a single Sunday.
  • College football ends in early December.
  • So between the Super Bowl / early January and the first spring-training pitch there is four to six weeks with nothing on the dial.

The catch

The window is real but it is not the whole year. Outside it: the MLB season runs late March → late September (plus October playoffs), the NFL runs early August → early January, and college football runs late August → late November. In the MLB season the only reliable breaks are the All-Star break (4–5 days) and Mondays/Thursdays when the Giants and A's are both off.

Practical consequence: a 2–3 week vacation is only possible in Jan–mid-Feb. A 1-week vacation is possible in Jan–mid-Feb or the All-Star break.

5. Things you should know before you book

  • 2028 and 2029 are not real yet. MLB, the NFL, Stanford and Cal have published nothing for those years. Their windows on this site are modelled from the released seasons and are labelled Estimated everywhere. They are stable enough for planning, but re-check once the real schedules drop (MLB usually ~10 months ahead, NFL in May).
  • 2027 is half real. MLB's full 2027 schedule is official (opening day 24 March 2027, All-Star 13 July 2027, regular season ends 26 September 2027). The NFL and both colleges have not released 2027.
  • “Audible” excludes Stanford and the Westwood One national feed because no official page confirms which Bay Area station carries them. If you know your Stanford station, add it and the Friday/Saturday picture changes.
  • Game lengths are estimates (MLB 165 min, NFL 192 min, college 206 min) and are editable in config/config.json.
  • Time zone. Every start time on this site is Pacific. The Westwood One pages publish Eastern times; they were converted, not copied.
