HOW TO READ THIS

“Free” means no tracked game is on air

01

Scheduled play

A game date is busy for vacation screening, even when kickoff or first pitch is not yet published. Exact busy hours need a verified start time and a measured/verified end.

02

Radio availability

Only a station’s published schedule or an official league/team broadcast listing can confirm carriage. Radio reception varies; web listings do not guarantee reception in Outer Sunset.

03

Unknown is not free

Unpublished dates/times and incomplete coverage are labeled unknown. We do not estimate future schedules or fill gaps with assumptions.
