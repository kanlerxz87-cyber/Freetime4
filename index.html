When is it free to take a vacation?

Plan your getaway around live sports on the radio in the San Francisco Bay Area (outer sunset, 94122). A minute is "free" when none of these are on air: MLB (Giants & A's high priority; all 30 clubs shown), NFL (49ers preseason/regular/postseason; all NFL), Stanford NCAAF, Cal NCAAF, and Westwood One national NFL radio broadcasts.

Executive Summary

Bottom line: There are no fully verified MLB, NFL, or NCAAF schedules for 2027, 2028, or 2029 yet — those have not been officially released. Everything below for 2027–2029 is an estimate based on typical calendar patterns and is not a verified released schedule. Verified 2026 data comes from official MLB.com, NFL.com/49ers.com, gostanford.com, calfootball.com, and Westwood One sources (linked on each page).

What "free" means

  • Free: no live MLB, NFL, Stanford NCAAF, Cal NCAAF, or Westwood One national NFL radio broadcast on air.
  • Not free (busy): any of the above games being broadcast live over the radio in the SF Bay Area.
  • High priority: San Francisco Giants, Oakland Athletics, San Francisco 49ers, Stanford Cardinal, Cal Golden Bears — plus every MLB club and every NFL game shown.
  • Timezone: PST / PDT (Pacific). All game times shown in Pacific.
