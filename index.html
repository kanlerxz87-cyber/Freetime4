FREE TIME VACATION

When can you leave the Bay Area without missing live sports on AM/FM radio? · MLG, NFL, Stanford/Cal NCAAF, Westwood One · Times in PST
THE ANSWER, UP FRONT. Across 2027–2029 there is exactly one stretch of the year when your core sports (all MLB, all NFL, Stanford & Cal football, Westwood One national NFL) go quiet: between the Super Bowl and the start of baseball. Under the strictest reading of your rules (MLB spring training counts as “MLB preseason”), the best clean week in 2027 is February 7–13, 2027. If exhibition spring-training games don’t count for you, the window explodes open to February 15 – March 23, 2027 (37 days) — enough for a 1-, 2-, or 3-week vacation. There is no 3-week window under the strict reading in any of 2027, 2028 or 2029.
