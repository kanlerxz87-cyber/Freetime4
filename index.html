When is it free to take a vacation?

Plan your getaway around live sports on the radio in the San Francisco Bay Area (outer sunset, 94122). A minute is free when none of these are on air: MLB (Giants & A's high priority; all 30 clubs shown), NFL (49ers preseason/regular/postseason; all NFL), Stanford NCAAF, Cal NCAAF, and Westwood One national NFL radio broadcasts.

Verification note

Schedule status varies by sport and year. MLB's 2027 regular-season schedule is officially released; MLB 2028–2029 and complete NFL/college schedules for future seasons are not. Estimates are labeled, unverified values are left blank and flagged, and unknown dates are never treated as free. See Vacation Windows and the Flagged for Review pages.
