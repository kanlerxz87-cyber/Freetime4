Westwood One Sports — national NFL radio

Every national broadcast window that reaches San Francisco on AM/FM (KNBR 680/104.5 · KTCT 1050)

Westwood One is the NFL's national radio partner: Thursday Night, Monday Night and Sunday Night Football, Thanksgiving and Christmas tripleheaders, Black Friday, Saturday double/tripleheaders, the full postseason and the Super Bowl. In San Francisco these are heard on KNBR 680 AM / 104.5 FM and KTCT 1050 AM — both verified SF affiliates (official station finder).

The official Westwood One schedule (read 2026-11-25, westwoodonesports.com/nfl-schedule) currently lists events through Week 18 (Jan 10, 2027). Postseason games are added to their calendar during the playoffs — modelled here as national windows with estimated times (F08). Their listed start times are broadcast starts and can precede kickoff by ~30 minutes (F07).
