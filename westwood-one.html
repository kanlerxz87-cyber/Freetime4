Westwood One Sports — National NFL Radio (Bay Area AM/FM)

Westwood One provides national radio play-by-play of NFL games. These broadcasts are carried on local Westwood One AM/FM affiliates in the San Francisco Bay Area and are receivable with a standard AM/FM radio in outer sunset SF (94122), subject to reception. When any of these is on air, that time is not free.

How this affects "free time"

  • Thursday, Sunday, and Monday night NFL games (and the full postseason) broadcast on Westwood One count as busy for free-time purposes.
  • Because Westwood One games are national, they can coincide with games involving teams other than the 49ers — so even a non-49ers NFL night can be "not free."
  • Times shown are Pacific; convert from ET by subtracting 3 hours (PST).
