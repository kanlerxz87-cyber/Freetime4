How free time is computed

  • Free window: a stretch of the day with no busy game block. Day considered 6:00 a.m. – 11:59 p.m. PT.
  • Busy block: a game on air. Verified games use exact official times. Estimated games use average durations (MLB ~165 min, NFL ~180 min, NCAAF ~180 min + halftime).
  • High priority: Giants, A's, 49ers, Stanford, Cal are marked high priority but count as busy just like any other game.
  • Westwood One: Thursday/Sunday/Monday night national NFL radio (and postseason) counted as busy during NFL season windows.

Nothing was inferred or filled in where an official source could not be confirmed; estimates are flagged ESTIMATE. See Flagged for Review.
