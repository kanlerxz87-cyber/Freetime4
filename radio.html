Radio — what's on your dial in 94122

The whole point: only count a game as a conflict if it is actually on an AM/FM radio you can receive in Outer Sunset. This page is the evidence trail for those stations.

The single most useful find. The 49ers' official 2026 schedule prints the radio outlet on every upcoming game card: KSAN 107.7 FM / KNBR 104.5 FM / 680 AM. Cal's official cards print KSFO 810 AM (and KNBR 104.5 FM / 680 AM for the Big Game). That is direct, official carriage evidence rather than a guess.
