Outer Sunset (94122) reception notes

KNBR 680 is a 50,000-watt Class A clear-channel signal — reliably receivable across San Francisco including the Outer Sunset. KTCT 1050 AM (10 kW night) and KNEW 960 AM are standard Bay Area AM signals. KSAN 107.7, KNBR-FM 104.5 and KGMZ 95.7 are full-market FM signals. All of the core flagships are ordinary AM/FM — no streaming subscription needed.

Carriage claims are only made where a team/station official page (or the official Westwood One station finder) confirms them. Unconfirmed items are on the Flagged for Review page.
