03 · MLB time-zone presentation

MLB’s schedule hub supplies official matchups and times as released. This project has not transcribed all 30 clubs’ future games into a Pacific-time event table. Dates without such transcription are unknown, not clear.

04 · Bay Area AM/FM carriage

National coverage on Westwood One does not by itself prove a particular Bay Area station carries a broadcast, or that the signal is receivable at 94122. Confirm station affiliation and its current published schedule before treating an event as radio-busy.

05 · Game duration and exact free hours

Typical durations are not fixed end times. Any duration-based daily free-time calculation would be an estimate and could misstate overlap. This version avoids invented end times and does not label portions of game days free.
