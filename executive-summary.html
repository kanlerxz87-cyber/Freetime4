Executive Summary — Best Times to Vacation (2026–2029)

Purpose: Identify when there are no live sports on the radio in the San Francisco Bay Area (outer sunset, 94122) so you can plan a vacation free of scheduling conflicts with the games you want to be home to listen to.

What counts as "free"

A minute is free when none of the following are on air:

  • MLB — all 30 clubs, with San Francisco Giants and Oakland Athletics flagged high priority.
  • NFL — San Francisco 49ers (preseason, regular season, postseason) flagged high priority; all NFL games shown.
  • Stanford NCAAF — high priority.
  • California (Cal) NCAAF — high priority.
  • Westwood One Sports national NFL radio broadcasts — Thursday Night, Sunday Night, Monday Night Football, Thanksgiving/Christmas, international games, and the entire postseason including the Super Bowl, carried on Bay Area AM/FM affiliates.

Timezone: PST / PDT (Pacific). Game times shown in Pacific.

Critical verification notice

As of 2026-11-25, official MLB, NFL, and NCAAF schedules for 2027, 2028, and 2029 have NOT been released. All 2027–2029 dates, windows, and game times in this summary are estimates based on typical calendar patterns. They are not verified released schedules. Do not book non-refundable travel based on these estimates until the official schedules are published. Only 2026 values carry official-source verification, and even then some fields are blank or flagged unverified.
